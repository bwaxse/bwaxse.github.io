I am a Med-Peds Infectious Disease fellow at [NIAID](https://www.niaid.nih.gov/)/[CNH](https://www.childrensnational.org/) conducting Electronic Health Records informatics research at the [NHGRI Center for Precision Health Research](https://www.genome.gov/about-nhgri/Division-of-Intramural-Research/Center-for-Precision-Health-Research).

Under [Josh Denny](https://www.genome.gov/staff/Joshua-Denny-MD-MS)'s mentorship, I develop methods to transform clinical data into research-grade insights about infectious diseases. My primary work uses the NIH _All of Us_ Research Program to study how genetics and clinical factors shape susceptibility to respiratory infections. Concurrent projects include integrating survey data in EHR phenotyping, time-bounded phenomic analyses, unit harmonization in _All of Us_, and ID-related phenome studies for NIAID collaborators.

Beyond research, I am board-certified in Internal Medicine, Pediatrics, and Adult Infectious Diseases, committed to advancing precision medicine through data science and advanced phenotyping. I am a Claude Code enthusiast eager to share my workflow and learnings along the way. I released [Scholia.fyi](https:scholia.fyi) in 2026 to help researchers use AI to critically evaluate primary literature. My other loves are family, cycling, yoga, music, and live shows.
