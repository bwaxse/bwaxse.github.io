I'm a Med-Peds Infectious Disease fellow at [NIAID](https://www.niaid.nih.gov/)/[CNH](https://www.childrensnational.org/) conducting genomics and EHR informatics research at the [NHGRI Center for Precision Health Research](https://www.genome.gov/about-nhgri/Division-of-Intramural-Research/Center-for-Precision-Health-Research).

Under [Josh Denny](https://www.genome.gov/staff/Joshua-Denny-MD-MS)'s mentorship, I study how genetics and clinical factors shape susceptibility to infectious diseases using large-scale datasets like the NIH *All of Us* Research Program. Current work includes KIR haplotype associations with severe COVID outcomes, long COVID phenotyping using EHR and survey data, and phenome-wide analyses for NIAID collaborators.

I'm board-certified in Internal Medicine, Pediatrics, and Adult Infectious Diseases. I built [Scholia.fyi](https://scholia.fyi) to help researchers use AI for critical evaluation of primary literature.

This blog documents how frontier models like Claude Code accelerate computational work for biomedical researchers—real workflows, actual code.

Outside work: family time with my wife and girls, cycling, yoga, live music.
